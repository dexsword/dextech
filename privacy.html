Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

1. Who We Are

Dex Tech ("we," "us," or "our") is a technology services company based in Henderson, NV. We provide in-home and on-site tech support, setup, and related services. You can reach us at dextech.me@gmail.com or (845) 596-1708.

2. Information We Collect

When you book an appointment through our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Requested service type
  • Any notes or details you provide about your request
  • Appointment date and time

We do not directly collect or store payment card information on our website or servers. Payments for subscription plans are processed through Stripe, and tips or donations may be sent via PayPal or cryptocurrency — all handled entirely by those external platforms under their own privacy policies. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics tools.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Schedule and confirm your appointment
  • Send you an SMS confirmation of your booking
  • Contact you if we need to reschedule or follow up on your appointment

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to your appointment.

4. SMS Messaging

By checking the SMS consent box during booking, you agree to receive text messages from Dex Tech at the phone number you provide. These messages are limited to appointment confirmations and direct follow-up related to your booking.

  • Message frequency: typically one message per booking
  • Message and data rates may apply
  • To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us
  • For help, reply HELP or contact us directly at (845) 596-1708

5. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your booking information is stored in a private database on our web server. This data is not publicly accessible and is only viewable by Dex Tech staff.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Twilio — delivers SMS appointment confirmations. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely to send your confirmation message. See Twilio's privacy policy.
  • Stripe — processes subscription plan payments. Payment card details are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • PayPal — used for optional tips and donations. Transactions are handled entirely by PayPal. See PayPal's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

7. Data Retention

We retain booking records for up to 2 years for business record-keeping purposes. If you would like your information removed sooner, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request a copy of the information we have on file for you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information

To make any of these requests, email dextech.me@gmail.com or call (845) 596-1708.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our booking service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
